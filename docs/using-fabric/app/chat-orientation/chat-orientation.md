Fabric lets you arrange your chat tabs in one of two layouts — a **horizontal** strip along the top (the default) or a **vertical** list down the left side. Both behave identically; only the arrangement changes, so you can pick whichever fits your screen and the way you like to work.

---

## Horizontal Layout (Default)

In the horizontal layout, your chat tabs sit in a strip across the **top** of the window, flowing left to right — the familiar browser-style tab bar. The full width of the window below is available for the chat and editor.

This works well on standard and laptop screens, where vertical space is precious and you only have a handful of tabs open at a time.

---

## Vertical Layout

In the vertical layout, your chat tabs move into a **fixed left-side panel**, stacking top to bottom. Each tab gets the full width of the panel, so longer tab names are easier to read, and a long list of chats scrolls vertically instead of overflowing off the edge of the screen.

This shines on wide and ultrawide monitors, and whenever you keep many chats open at once — a vertical list is far easier to scan than a crowded horizontal strip.

---

## Switching Layouts

You change the layout in **Settings → General**, in the **Chat Management** section. Look for the toggle labeled **Vertical tabs (left side panel)**:

- **Off** (default) — horizontal tab strip along the top
- **On** — vertical tab panel on the left

The change applies immediately, and tab groups and drag-and-drop work exactly the same in both layouts.

---

## Which Should You Use?

- **Horizontal** — best for laptops and standard displays, or if you usually keep just a few chats open.
- **Vertical** — best for wide/ultrawide monitors, or if you run many chats in parallel and want a readable, scannable list.

There's no wrong choice — try both and keep whichever feels more comfortable. You can switch back at any time.
